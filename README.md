# Pink LSZ Plugin
A plugin for the Pink LSZ clan. It records your drops, kill counts, collection log, combat achievements, personal bests and deaths as you play, and sends them to the clan dashboard (https://pinkosrs.com) for leaderboards, boss stats, member profiles and clan events.

Only current clan members are accepted. Install it, log in, and add your RuneScape name to your dashboard profile.

## What it sends
- Drops, kill counts, collection log and combat achievements
- Personal bests, with a screenshot, and every other finished boss or raid time (used for clan bingo speed tiles)
- Deaths at bosses and raids, with a screenshot (can be turned off)
- Screenshots of valuable drops and of items the clan is watching for during events

Everything goes to the dashboard over HTTPS and nowhere else, along with your character name and account hash. Screenshots can show public chat; private messages are hidden.

## TeamFinder
Click the Pink LSZ icon in the RuneLite sidebar to find or make a team for group content (Chambers of Xeric, Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut, The Fractured Archive, the God Wars bosses, Nex, Royal Titans, Yama and more).
- Pick an activity, mode and team size (or Mass), add an optional note, and create the team. Others see it in the panel and in the clan Discord.
- Game-chat lines tell you when someone joins or leaves, when the host changes, and when the team is full and which world to hop to.
- If the host leaves, the next player to have joined takes over. A player whose plugin goes quiet for 45 seconds is removed from the team.
- Clan moderators can remove a team.

## Clan announcements
Messages leaders send from the dashboard appear in your chatbox.

## Settings
- **Dashboard address**: leave as `https://pinkosrs.com`.
- **Show messages in chat**: a line when something is sent.
- **Show clan announcements**: leader messages in chat (on by default).
- **Hide Side Panel**: removes the TeamFinder button from the sidebar (off by default).
- Options to turn off death tracking and gear sharing.
